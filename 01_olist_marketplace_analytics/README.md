## Project 01: Olist Marketplace Intelligence & Customer Analytics

### Research Overview

This project investigates the Brazilian e-commerce marketplace using the publicly available Olist dataset.

The objective is to analyze business performance, customer behavior, delivery operations, and customer satisfaction, then develop predictive models and evidence-based business recommendations.

### Dataset

- Source: [Brazilian E-Commerce Public Dataset by Olist](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/olistbr/brazilian-ecommerce)
- Data format: CSV
- Coverage: Historical Brazilian e-commerce transactions (2016–2018)
- Includes customer, order, product, seller, payment, delivery, and review information.

The original dataset is downloaded separately and is not included in this repository.

### Research Questions

1. What factors influence customer satisfaction?
2. Which product categories generate the most revenue?
3. Where do delivery delays occur most frequently?
4. Can machine learning help identify orders at risk of late delivery?
5. What improvements can help businesses make better operational decisions?
