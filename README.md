# Business Intelligence & Analytics Lab

**Transforming Data into Insights, Predictions, and Smarter Business Decisions.**

## About

An applied research portfolio exploring Business Intelligence, Data Analytics, Predictive Modeling, and Management Science through real-world datasets.

## Project 01: Olist Marketplace Intelligence & Customer Analytics

An analytical study of the Brazilian e-commerce marketplace using the public Olist dataset.

### Research Objectives

- Analyze business performance and revenue.
- Investigate customer purchasing behavior.
- Evaluate delivery and logistics performance.
- Study factors associated with customer satisfaction.
- Develop predictive machine-learning models.
- Provide evidence-based business recommendations.

### Dataset

Source: [Brazilian E-Commerce Public Dataset by Olist](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/olistbr/brazilian-ecommerce)

Historical e-commerce data from 2016–2018, including customers, orders, products, sellers, payments, deliveries, and reviews.

### Technologies

Python · Pandas · NumPy · SQL · SQLAlchemy · Scikit-learn · Matplotlib · Seaborn
